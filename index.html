How it plays

  • One finger: hold to hook and swing, release to fly
  • Fly close to a pillar for a NEAR MISS and watch your hero show off
  • Hook again in mid-air for AIR COMBOs, land in the middle for PERFECT
  • Every hero has a skill and a journey through five painted worlds
  • Seasonal events with heroes you can only win then
